## Primary framework references

The RICE calculation is Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Its inputs and dependency caveat are checked against [Intercom's RICE article](https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/). Our worked inputs are explicitly hypothetical.

MoSCoW distinguishes essential, important, optional and excluded scope **for a particular increment**. Its rules and effort-based contingency are checked against [Agile Business Consortium's MoSCoW guidance](https://www.agilebusiness.org/resource/what-is-moscow-prioritization/).

The North Star should connect customer value, product activity and business direction. The outcome-oriented approach is informed by [Amplitude's North Star Playbook](https://amplitude.com/books/north-star). Our metric definition is a project proposal, not an Amplitude recommendation for kitchens.

## Evidence labels

**Observed:** directly witnessed or reported in an identifiable real research session. **Inferred:** interpretation linked to such observations. **Hypothesized:** untested customer/problem/solution proposition. **Historical:** dated or superseded project decision. **External:** educational or secondary source. **User decision:** explicit product constraint from this conversation. **Engineering verified:** software behavior checked with a reproducible test; does not imply product value.
